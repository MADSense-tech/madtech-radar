Fox completes its $22B acquisition of Roku

M&A, TechCrunch, 2026-11-06, Fox, Roku, Tubi

Fox closed its roughly $22B purchase of Roku after DOJ clearance, months ahead of its first-half 2027 target. Roku comes off Nasdaq and joins Tubi, Fox One and Red Seat Ventures in a new Fox Digital Platforms division. Fox has promised to keep Roku neutral for at least three years, with data firewalls for content partners.

A broadcaster now owns one of the largest US CTV operating systems and ad platforms, reaching over 100M households, which changes how buyers and DSPs get access to CTV inventory and data.

Can web users trust ad tech's server-side shift?

Audience data, Digiday, 2026-11-10, IAB Tech Lab, Apple

After Safari's identity crackdown, publishers are moving ad calls server-side, led by IAB Tech Lab's open-source Trusted Server framework. Its edge cookie hashes the user's IP with a domain-specific secret key, so it can survive cookie clearing, which raises questions about user privacy controls.

Server-side IDs controlled by publishers are becoming the main workaround for browser signal loss, and they are likely to draw privacy and regulatory scrutiny.
