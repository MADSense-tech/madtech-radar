Apple's German ATT deal lets apps re-ask users who already said no

Audience data, PPC Land, 2026-11-13, Apple

Under commitments made binding by Germany's Bundeskartellamt, Apple will redesign the App Tracking Transparency prompt for German users: it drops the word 'track', matches Apple's own Personalized Ads prompt, and lets apps ask again users who already declined, plus once a year after any answer. Changes are due within about four months and the commitments run for seven years.

This is the first regulator-forced change to ATT and could raise iOS opt-in rates and IDFA availability in a major EU market, giving other regulators a model to follow.
