Adobe Integrates Agentic CX Enterprise Coworker Into AI Platforms from Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and More

MCP, Adobe Newsroom, 2026-11-04, Adobe, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Databricks, Snowflake, Amazon Ads, Meta, Demandbase, IBM

Adobe's CX Enterprise Coworker agent now runs inside ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini for business, Microsoft Copilot and Databricks Genie, with Amazon Quick, IBM and Snowflake listed as coming soon. The agent is built on MCP and A2A, and Adobe shipped prebuilt MCP connectors for Amazon Ads, Meta's ads MCP server, Snowflake Cortex AI, Databricks Genie, Demandbase and others. Major holding companies are building custom implementations.

The largest marketing-cloud vendor is putting its orchestration layer inside general AI assistants and connecting it to ad platforms' own MCP servers, so the marketer's main interface moves from Adobe's UI to whichever assistant the client already uses.

As Publicis closes LiveRamp deal, some brands say they're jumping ship

M&A, Marketing Dive, 2026-11-03, Publicis Groupe, LiveRamp, Epsilon, Liquid Death, WPP, Omnicom

Publicis completed its roughly $2.2B acquisition of LiveRamp on Nov 2, under an FTC consent order meant to keep LiveRamp neutral. Liquid Death's media chief said publicly that the brand will leave LiveRamp, and WPP and Omnicom have signaled for months that they will wind down their use of it.

The industry's most widely used neutral identity and data collaboration layer is now owned by an agency holding company, so buyers and sellers who rely on RampID and LiveRamp clean rooms may start moving to alternatives.

The Saudi-led consortium that bought EA is in talks to acquire Unity, says Reuters

M&A, MobileGamer.biz (citing Reuters), 2026-11-06, Unity, Silver Lake, Saudi Public Investment Fund (PIF), confidence: medium

Reuters reported on Nov 5 that Silver Lake, which already owns about 8% of Unity, and Saudi Arabia's PIF have approached Unity about a takeover valuing it at around $25B. The talks are early and no deal is certain. Unity's Vector ad platform recently helped it post its first profitable quarter since its IPO.

A take-private would put one of the largest mobile in-app ad platforms, a direct rival to AppLovin, under private-equity and sovereign-fund ownership and could change competition in mobile performance advertising.

Attain enhances the power of purchase data across Amazon Ads DVA+ with direct audience integration

Audience data, GlobeNewswire (Attain press release); also covered by Adweek and PPC Land, 2026-11-04, Attain, Amazon Ads, Publicis Groupe, Dentsu, Tombras

Attain's purchase-based audiences, drawn from a permissioned panel of more than 14 million consumers, can now be built and activated natively in Amazon Ads DVA+ (formerly Amazon DSP). Segments can be cut by retailer, category and online versus in-store purchase, and refresh daily. DVA+ is still in US closed beta with general availability targeted for Q1 2027, and pricing was not disclosed.

Amazon's DSP can now target shoppers by what they buy at Walmart, Kroger and other retailers, which strengthens its pitch to brands that don't sell on Amazon.

Supermetrics Advances Marketing AI from Analysis to Approved Campaign Action Through Claude

MCP, PR Newswire (Supermetrics press release), 2026-11-03, Supermetrics, Anthropic, Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok, LinkedIn, Snapchat

Supermetrics' Claude connector can now create and edit campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok, LinkedIn and Snapchat, not just analyze them. New campaigns stay paused until a person approves them, and a new Context Layer stores team naming and reporting rules. More than 6,000 customers have used the connector since its February launch.

Agents are moving from reporting to making changes in live ad accounts across platforms, which makes approval gates and governance a key buying criterion and competes with the platforms' own MCP servers.

Titan OS raises $46 million to expand its smart TV OS internationally

M&A, TechCrunch, 2026-11-04, Titan OS, BlackRock, Highland Europe, Mangrove Capital Partners, Philips

Smart TV operating system maker Titan OS raised $46M in equity and debt, with debt from BlackRock-managed funds and money from existing investors Highland Europe and Mangrove, bringing total funding to $110M. Its user base grew from 18M to 30M in a year, and it opened a London office focused on advertising.

TV operating systems are where CTV ad supply and audience data start, and a well-funded independent alternative to Google TV and Roku gives buyers another source of CTV inventory.

Future & InfoSum bring audience data to WPP planners

Partnerships, IT Brief, 2026-11-06, Future, InfoSum, WPP, WPP Media

Audience data from Future's Aperture engine, built from first-party data across more than 170 brands such as TechRadar and Tom's Guide, is now available inside WPP's Open Intelligence through InfoSum's collaboration layer. WPP is building its own publisher data supply on InfoSum just as Publicis takes control of LiveRamp, adding to the split of clean room infrastructure along holding-company lines.

Publica & Optable Help CTV Publishers Enrich Bid Signals

Audience data, TV News Check, 2026-11-05, Publica, Integral Ad Science, Optable, LiveRamp, ID5, UID2

Publica, which IAS owns, has integrated Optable's ID Switchboard so CTV and mobile in-app video publishers can map their audiences to identifiers buyers already use, such as RampID, ID5 and UID2, without building a separate integration for each. Publishers choose which buyers receive the enriched signals, and one large US publisher reported about a 20% eCPM lift over inventory without IDs.

On the sell side, CTV identity is becoming a translation layer across multiple IDs rather than a bet on a single one.

Hightouch Launches Match Booster in Europe, Raising Match Rates Without Compromising Compliance

Audience data, Business Wire (via FinancialContent), 2026-11-03, Hightouch, Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, The Trade Desk, Snapchat

Hightouch launched Match Booster in the UK, Germany, France, Spain and Italy. It adds identifiers from a GDPR-compliant European identity graph to consented first-party audiences as they sync to more than 50 ad platforms, with data processed in-region. Hightouch says a pilot matched 15% or more above the client's existing onboarding provider.

Warehouse-native activation tools are competing directly with traditional onboarders such as LiveRamp, now owned by Publicis, on match rates in Europe's strict privacy markets.
