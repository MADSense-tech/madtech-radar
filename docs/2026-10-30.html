DoorDash to Acquire Instacart to Help Local Stores Win

M&A, DoorDash, 2026-10-29, DoorDash, Instacart

DoorDash agreed to acquire Instacart for $65.00 per share, about $17B in equity value and a 26% premium, with at least 75% paid in cash backed by a JPMorgan bridge loan. Instacart's ad and other revenue rose 23% to $331M in Q3. The deal needs shareholder approval and antitrust clearance in the US, Canada and Israel, and is expected to close between late 2027 and the first half of 2028.

It would combine two delivery-app retail media networks with over $1B in ad revenue each, consolidating grocery and convenience commerce media into one large seller and changing how CPG brands split retail media budgets.

New products, Google Ads & Commerce Blog, 2026-10-28, Google

Google is bringing ads in AI Mode to Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and Singapore, with Brazil, Mexico and the UK planned for Q4 2026. New products, AdExchanger, 2026-10-28, Meta, Nvidia

Meta announced an LLM-based ad retrieval and ranking system, an upgraded GEM model that reads twice as much user history, and GenRecs, which describes ads by attributes such as brand, color and style to help new ads get started. Apple's Safari crackdown pushes ad tech toward server-side solutions, but doubts remain

Audience data, Digiday, 2026-10-28, Apple, ID5, LiveRamp, Permutive, The Trade Desk, Audigent, IAB Tech Lab

Following WebKit's block on identity resolution vendors including UID2, ID5, LiveRamp, Permutive and Audigent, ID5 is moving more of its identity setup server-side and testing revised integrations with publishers, and interest in IAB Tech Lab's Trusted Server Framework is rising despite complexity and fingerprinting concerns. Permutive says only a few customers were affected and moved them to a stopgap. Related Digiday data shows iOS bid rates down about 8% and Mac bid rates down about 25%.

With WebKit browsers carrying about 30% of publisher impressions, server-side identity is becoming the default workaround, but it may trigger another round of browser blocks.
