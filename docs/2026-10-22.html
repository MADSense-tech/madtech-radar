Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Braze

M&A, Braze (press release), 2026-10-20, Salesforce, Braze

Salesforce agreed to acquire customer engagement platform Braze for $47.50 per share in cash, an enterprise value of about $5.66B and a premium of roughly 58%. Braze's real-time messaging and AI decisioning will be combined with Marketing Cloud, Data 360 and Agentforce. Closing is expected in Salesforce's fiscal 2028, subject to Braze shareholder and regulatory approval.

Salesforce is admitting that Marketing Cloud's batch-based design falls short and buying real-time engagement instead, which removes a major independent engagement platform and raises the stakes for Adobe, Iterable and Klaviyo.

Blocking Identity Resolution Infrastructure

Audience data, WebKit Blog (Apple), 2026-10-19, Apple, LiveRamp, The Trade Desk, ID5, Permutive, Experian

Apple's WebKit team confirmed that Safari and WebKit in the iOS, iPadOS, macOS and visionOS 27 releases block network access to third parties that turn email addresses into persistent cross-site IDs. Blocking is by domain and the post names no vendors. Digiday reports that LiveRamp, UID2, ID5, Permutive and Audigent (Experian) domains are affected, that The Trade Desk's ad server was blocked by mistake and has since been restored, and that Apple's growing ads business has executives questioning its motives.

Identity built on hashed emails, the main fallback after cookies, no longer works on Safari, so reach, frequency and measurement plans need a fallback that doesn't rely on IDs, and Apple's own ad ambitions may draw competition scrutiny.

FTC Order Preserves Competition in Digital Advertising Ecosystem as Part of Publicis's Acquisition of LiveRamp

M&A, Federal Trade Commission, 2026-10-19, Publicis Groupe, LiveRamp, WPP, Omnicom

The FTC voted 2-0 to accept a proposed consent order that clears Publicis's roughly $2.2B purchase of LiveRamp. For five years, Publicis must provide nondiscriminatory access to LiveRamp products and keep firewalls around customer data, verified by independent audits. The order is open for public comment for 30 days, and Publicis expects to close by year-end. Marketing Dive reports that WPP is winding down its agencies' use of LiveRamp and that Omnicom has moved up its exit.

These neutrality rules will set the template for agency-owned identity and clean-room infrastructure, and rival holding companies leaving LiveRamp anyway could split identity partners along agency lines.
